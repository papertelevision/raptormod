## PROMPT

Create a game HUD lower control panel in the same steampunk style as the attached image: aged
copper and brass, riveted plates, small pipes and gears, dark metal recesses, warm highlights.
Keep the same simplified, horizontal-bar look. This is a REDESIGN OF THE LAYOUT, so follow these
rules exactly.

CANVAS AND FRAMING
- Landscape, the widest format available. The panel is one long horizontal bar that runs the
  FULL width of the image, cut off at the left and right edges, sitting in the lower part of the
  canvas.
- Background: pure flat black (#000000) everywhere outside the panel. No shadows, glow, haze,
  smoke, vignette or gradients on the black. No floor, no scenery.
- No text, letters, numbers, labels or logos anywhere.

THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE: WHERE THINGS MAY GO
Measure across the full width of the bar, from 0% at the left edge to 100% at the right edge.
- ALL buttons, keys, screens and the health slot must sit between 15% and 85% of the width.
  Nothing interactive may be placed in the outer 15% on either side.
- The outer 15% on each side is DECORATION ONLY. It holds:
    1. a decorative end cap (gears, pipes, bolts) right at the outer edge, about the outer 8%;
    2. between that end cap and the first control, a PLAIN STRETCH of straight horizontal rails
       or pipes, about 5–7% wide. This stretch must contain only straight, uniform, horizontal
       elements: no bolts, rivets, joints, gauges or ornaments. It has to look right when it is
       repeated or trimmed sideways, so the panel can fit any screen width.
- The exact centre (50%) is a seam where the panel splits into two halves for an animation. Put
  a plain vertical joint there. Nothing small or detailed may straddle the centre line.

LAYOUT, LEFT TO RIGHT (all inside 15%–85%)
1. INVENTORY SCREEN: a rectangular screen about 17% of the width, in a riveted bezel. The inside
   is a completely flat, uniform, matte surface in one colour (muted pale green-grey), with no
   texture, stains, scratches, reflections or noise, because text is drawn on it.
2. Directly right of it, a narrow vertical column with two small integrated keys: UP arrow above,
   DOWN arrow below.
3. Two square mode buttons, side by side: the first shows a crosshair/target icon (scan), the
   second a folded-map icon (map). Integrated into the panel, not square buttons in round holes.
4. HEALTH SLOT: a long, thin, horizontal recessed slot, about 13% of the width, empty and dark
   inside (uniform near-black, no texture). A bar is drawn into it by the game. The centre seam
   (50%) may pass through this slot.
5. One small square button with a gear icon (menu).
6. LOG / DIALOGUE SCREEN: a wide rectangular screen, about 30% of the width, in a riveted bezel.
   The inside is completely flat, uniform, matte dark charcoal, with no texture or noise, because
   text is drawn on it.
7. Directly right of it, inside the 85% limit, a narrow vertical key column: an EXPAND key with a
   double chevron pointing UP at the top, then an UP arrow key, then a DOWN arrow key.

SIZE AND PROPORTION
- The main body of the bar is a uniform horizontal band about one tenth as tall as the image is
  wide. The end caps may rise higher above the band at the outer edges, like the attached image.
- Both text screens should be as tall as the band allows: their flat interiors should take up at
  least 60% of the band's height.
- Keep every element crisp and readable at small size. Clean silhouette, solid metal edges, no
  feathered or smoky edges against the black.
