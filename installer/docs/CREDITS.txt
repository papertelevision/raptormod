RaptorMod {VERSION} -- credits
=============================

RaptorMod: Josh Sears -- design, art direction, remastered textures and
artwork, soundtrack recordings, testing.

Azrael's Tear (1996) was developed by Intelligent Games and published by
Mindscape. RaptorMod patches the player's own copy; it contains none of the
original game's program or data files.

Built with:
  - DOSBox Staging (https://dosbox-staging.github.io) -- the emulator
  - GeneralUser GS by S. Christian Collins -- the General MIDI soundfont
  - Real-ESRGAN (Xintao Wang et al.) -- used to upscale textures
